# Music Lessons by NXD

Website and online school platform for Music Lessons by NXD — guitar, piano and vocal lessons.

- YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCEenfAl8MxUJ3vQEqyCmXCA
- LinkedIn: https://in.linkedin.com/company/music-lessons-by-nxd
- Site: https://nxdtribe.com/

## Planned scope

**Public site:** welcome + WhatsApp CTA, client feedback & videos, meet the team, store, contact form, blog, Instagram & YouTube feeds, student showcase, WhatsApp group, subscriptions (Free, Batch monthly, 1-on-1 × 4, 1-on-1 × 8), signup/login.

**Client portal:** getting-started lessons (guitar/piano/vocals), profile & student info, payments, subscription, calendar with Google Meet links (read-only for free accounts), lesson list, assignments, progress and class videos.

**Admin:** payments, scheduling, classes, curriculum & lesson plans, feedback, pricing/beta pricing/discounts, website content, blog writing & scheduled publishing, per-student monthly lesson and payment tracking.
